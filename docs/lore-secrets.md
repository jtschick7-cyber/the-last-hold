## 1. Theme in one line

> **What we burn to keep ourselves safe becomes the dark we fear. What we remember keeps us warm.**

- **The order's sin was burning people to stay safe.**
- **The player's way out is remembering:** the bestiary, the recipe book, the letters, and the names. The "Knowledge is progression" pillar (GDD §1.3) is the story's answer.
- **The tone stays heroic with dark edges.** The dead were brave, the betrayers had reasons, and nobody is a cartoon villain, not even the thing beneath.

## 3. The Emberwrights

### 3.1 The order

- An order of **forge-smiths who fought with fire**: forge-heated blades, pitch, and runes that burn. They forged **silvered and runed steel**.
- **The full vow:** *"The fire for the folk; the folk never for the fire."* Only the first clause was ever spoken in public. The second is the one the order broke.
- **Ranks:** Apprentice → Wright → Master-Wright. At the top stand the **Hearthblade** (field champion) and the **Keeper of the Forge** ("the Keeper": head of the order, guardian of the Deep Forge) *(⚑ L7: renamed from Forge-Mother/Forge-Father)*.
- **Symbol:** an anvil with a flame rising from it in place of a hammer.
- **The ribbon:** a blue ribbon with a stitched hem, given to every new apprentice. Zlata wove the first ones as a true honor. During the Sending, the ribbon marked the Sent.
- **The Kamenfell outpost:** a training forge in the fells. Every apprentice spent a year there before being "sent onward" to Stanholm.

### 3.2 The Hearthseal

Beneath Stanholm is the **Deep Forge**, a chasm where the founders found that the cold thing below recoils from forge-fire. Over it they built the **Hearthseal**, a vast runed hearth that burns forever.

**Ordinary fire wasn't enough.** The seal burns **embers**: the order's word for the distilled warmth of a life, meaning its memories, its loves, and what it meant.
- Every founding Wright swore to give their own ember at death. **"We are the fuel."**
- That was willing, noble, and terrible.

## 4. The full truth

1. **The founding (Y0).** For about 180 years, willing Wrights' embers are enough.
2. **The thinning (Y180).** Wrights die far from the hold, and their embers are lost.
3. **The Sending (Y200)** *(⚑ L7: renamed from "the Tithe")*:
   - A Keeper begins **sending apprentices onward**: one every second spring, 16 to 18 years old, chosen from the villages in turn and celebrated with a feast and the ribbon.
   - The villages believe the apprentices go on to distant forges.
   - **In truth, each one is carried into the Deep Forge from Kamenfell and given to the seal.**
   - The ledger records them with one cold word: **"onward."**
   - Only the Keeper and a handful of Master-Wrights know.
4. **Dobrava becomes Keeper (Y260).** She inherits the Sending and hates it. For twenty years she searches for another fuel and fails. **She keeps it running, because the alternative is the dark loose in the Gloamreach.** *Few to save many.*
5. **Sigrun runs (Y278).** On the night she is to be Sent, she sees what waits in the Deep Forge and flees. Seal-fire takes her hand. She hides in the fells.
6. **Yrsa finds the ledger (Y280).** The Hearthblade finds her brother **Eirik**'s name marked *onward*, eight years old.
7. **The Night of the Fall (Y280).**
   - Yrsa and a few loyal Wrights, **Arvid Netmender** among them, go down and **break the Sending-chains**: the rune-chains that draw embers from the living.
   - The seal, no longer fed, gutters.
   - **The thing beneath rises.** Stanholm falls in one night, and most of the order dies.
8. **Dobrava's choice.** She could restart the chains with the surviving Wrights. **She doesn't.** Instead she **binds the seal to one office: warden of Stanholm.**
   - Whoever keeps the Hearth Hall keeps the seal.
   - When a warden falls, their ember feeds the seal. The tempered remainder **passes to a new warden**, along with what the last one knew.
   - **Dobrava was the first warden.** She burns every Sending record she can reach.
9. **Dobrava's vigil (Y280–Y300).** Alone in the ruin with Pechko, she keeps the seal alight for twenty years, and it uses up **her whole ember**. She dies at 78.
10. **The new wardens (Y300 on; L4).**
    - When a warden falls, the ember chooses **someone in the Gloamreach who has lost something to the dark**. They wake with warm hands and walk to Stanholm. **The player names each warden.**
    - **Why cycle 1 starts with zero Embers:** Dobrava's ember was spent completely on her vigil. Embers carry over only from the player's own fallen wardens.
    - **The Embers prestige currency is literally that:** the tempered warmth of your earlier wardens.

### 4.1 The deepest layer (revealed last)

**The Hearthseal and the thing beneath are one loop.**
- Every ember burned leaves **dross**: warmth turned to cold, memory turned to forgetting. *(⚑ L6: "dross," the slag a forge leaves behind, replaces "ash.")*
- The dross sinks into the Deep and **is the Unlit.** The founders found a small cold thing. **Three hundred years of burning grew it.**
- So the Sending made the problem worse, and the warden cycle still does, more slowly.
- **The Unlit isn't evil.** It's the cold left behind when someone is burned and forgotten. **It wants to be remembered, not fed.**

### 4.2 Who betrayed whom

| Who | The betrayal | Their reason | How the story treats them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yrsa Varn** (Hearthblade) | Broke the seal; caused the Fall | Her brother, and every Sent child | A **hero who made a catastrophic choice** and died in the Deep trying to make it right |
| **Dobrava Halsten** (Keeper) | Kept the Sending; buried the truth | Few to save many; then shame | A **tragic keeper** who paid with a 20-year vigil and her whole ember |
| **The order** | Broke its vow's second clause | Fear of the dark | The real fall happened a century before the Night |
| **Askel and Zlata Vel** | Built a seal that burns people (willingly, at first) | They didn't know about the dross | The **original mistake**, made in good faith |

There is no single traitor, only a chain of people choosing safety over the folk.

## 6. Endings: The Remembering

*⚑ Revised (lore B1).* "Remember Them" defines the set. The outcome depends on what you've remembered, plus one choice.

| Ending | Available | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Remember Them** (true) | All **40 names** recovered **and** the bestiary complete (§8) | The warden reads every Sent name aloud in the Hearth Hall, and the villages remember them. The Unlit, finally remembered, grows warm and small, and the seal no longer needs fuel. **The vow's second clause is kept at last (L2).** |
| **The Descent** | Any time | Take the fight **down into the Deep**: one final battle using everything learned across every cycle. A costly, heroic victory. The Unlit is scattered, not ended. |
| **Keep the Vigil** | Any time | Continue the cycle as an endless mode with rising difficulty. Bittersweet. |

**Why the willing Wrights aren't required:** their names are already cut into the **Roll of Honor** that the Keep Rank-up restores. Only the Sent were erased, so only their 40 names have to be found.

| Hunter | Available by | Needed for |
|---|---|---|
| Ostap | First successful contract (Hamlet) | Present throughout |

**The roster pattern** (lore C5): every hunter has a home band, a one-line backstory, and optionally a personal link to the Sending or the Fall. **Personal letters unlock at levels 5 and 8 (the highest level ever reached), from v0.5 on** *(D155: was 5 and 10)*. Five of them carry names.
