## 6. Endings: The Remembering

*⚑ Revised (lore B1).* "Remember Them" defines the set. The outcome depends on what you've remembered, plus one choice.

| Ending | Available | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Remember Them** (true) | All **40 names** recovered **and** every contract species at Studied (§8, D449) | The warden reads every Sent name aloud in the Hearth Hall, and the villages remember them. The Unlit, finally remembered, grows warm and small, and the seal no longer needs fuel. **The vow's second clause is kept at last (L2).** |
| **The Descent** | Any time | Take the fight **down into the Deep**: one final battle using everything learned across every cycle. A costly, heroic victory. The Unlit is scattered, not ended. |
| **Keep the Vigil** | When Remember Them is open (D459) | Not yet: the game goes on as it is, and Remember Them stays open. Rising difficulty is deferred (D453). |

v0.11 ships Remember Them and Keep the Vigil (D448). The Descent is deferred to its own flesh-out; the closing scene does not promise it.

**Why the willing Wrights aren't required:** their names are already cut into the **Roll of Honor** that the Keep Rank-up restores. Only the Sent were erased, so only their 40 names have to be found.

## 7. What survives the Long Night, in-world

| Thing | Persists? | In-world reason |
|---|---|---|
| Buildings, resources | Reset | The hold falls in the Last Stand. The next warden rebuilds from the ruins. |
| Threat | Reset | The fallen warden's ember feeds the seal, and the dark recedes for a while. |
| Hunter roster (active and reserve) | Persists | Hunters survive the stand, scatter to the villages, and come back when the new warden lights the hearth. |
| Hunter rank | Partly resets | Three years of rust and old wounds |
| Hunter gear | Resets | Broken or lost in the stand |
| Items (v0.8) | Reset, except Legendaries | Broken or lost in the stand, as gear always was |
| **Legendaries** (v0.8) | Persist | Like relics, objects that held a warden's warmth; each stays with its hunter |
| Hunter ranks, stars and talents (v0.8) | Persist | What a hunter has become doesn't rust in three years; only the edge does (levels) |
| Residents | Persist and age | Pechko is the hearth. Brana and Ilse wait out the gap in Velkvik. |
| Bestiary, recipe book, documents, names | Persist | Ilse's archive is kept away from the hold |
| Warden skills | Persist | Carried in the ember |
| **Relics** | Persist | **Objects that absorbed a falling warden's ember during the Last Stand.** The seal's warmth shields them, and the next warden finds them in the ruins, still warm. **[JC]** |
| Embers | Persist | Your fallen wardens' tempered warmth |
| Village trust | Persists and evolves | Three years of memory; each Triumphant stand raises it |
| **Roll of Wardens** (L9) | Persists | Each warden's name, Ranks reached, Last Stand band and relics, carved into the Hearth Hall wall |

## 8. The true-ending gate

**Rule (lore C3):** no required name or bestiary entry can be **Delve-only, event-only (world boss or Legendary Hunt), or missable.**

**The 40 names:**

| Source | Names | Why it can't be missed |
|---|---|---|
| Act documents (contracts, Last Stand milestones) | 28 (I 4 · II 6 · III 5 · IV 6 · V 4 · VI 3) | Their drops repeat until found. Earlier acts' documents stay obtainable. |
| **Ribbonshade** encounters (ruin contracts) | 6 | Each Ribbonshade carries one ribbon with a name, and ruin contracts recur until all six are found |
| Hunter letters (levels 5 and 8, D155) | 5 | Carried by story hunters, who can't die (§10) |
| Sigrun's confession | 1 | Her own name |

- **The Names view** (the Archivist) shows **found / total**, plus a where-to-look hint for every missing name. For example: *"A mother in Mirograd still writes to the hold."*
- **The bestiary requirement** counts every species that appears in contracts or the Last Stand (Studied, 4 kills; D449). Delve-only species, if any, are excluded. **[JC]** The Dross-Tide in a stand, the Great Beasts and the hunt quarries are optional and never count (D363, D365).
- **Every bestiary entry has a "What it was" line** (see the primer, §6.4). Completing the bestiary is itself an act of remembering.
