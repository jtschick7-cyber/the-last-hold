# v1.9 "Kettle & Fang" — summary for the owner (2026-11-17)

## What this milestone does

Milestone v1.9 completes all five monster families in **Events** by adding two new **Great Beasts**, and delivers a comprehensive **Alchemy & General Balance Pass** — making every bomb and every potion genuinely competitive alongside **Fire Pot**, **Frost Pot**, and **Quickfoot Tincture** while tightening combat honesty and tight simulator margins.

- **Two New Great Beasts (5 of 5 Monster Families Covered, `D631`):**
  - ***The Thorn-Stag*** (*Field & Forest Spirits*, `4th` in `boss.order`): base Might `42`, Armor `11`, Speed `10`, Atk `1`; Steel attacks; **weak to Steel (`1.5×`) & Fire (`1.25×`)** (resistant to Frost `0.75`, Venom `0.5`). *Thorn-Snare* holds the fastest hunter every 3rd action; *Bramble-Sweep* lashes all standing hunters every 4th action with direct Steel hits reduced by Armor. Drops **Thornwood** (`3` per milestone, `6` on kill) and unlocks the permanent ***Thorn-Antler Rack*** Courtyard Mark (`16 × 16 px`).
  - ***The Beacon-Bane*** (*Cursed*, `5th` in `boss.order`): base Might `44`, Armor `9`, Speed `9`, Atk `2`; **direct Fire attacks** (first world boss dealing Fire damage!); **weak to Rune (`1.5×`), Frost (`1.25×`), & Venom (`1.25×`)** (resistant to Steel `0.75`, Fire `0.25`). *Pitch-Flare* scorches every standing hunter for `1.5%` max Health Fire damage each action (`taken%` applies); *Curse-Brand* cuts healing received by `50%` on the highest-Might hunter for `2` actions every 4th action. Drops **Noon poppy** (`3` per milestone, `6` on kill) and unlocks the permanent ***Beacon-Iron Urn*** Courtyard Mark (`16 × 16 px`).
  - **Safe Save Schema `16 → 17` Migration:** Players at `visit 0..2` meet *The Thorn-Stag* cleanly at `visit 3`; saves at `visit 3` with no active fight keep `visit 3` (`The Thorn-Stag`) as their immediate next boss; saves past `visit 3` or mid-visit on pass `1+` remap cleanly (`pass × 5 + index`) without shifting active bosses, tiers, or falsely unlocking Courtyard Marks.
  - **1 Zughy-32 Art Review Batch (`T19.6`, `≤ 12` subjects per `D475`):** `2` Option B portraits (`64 × 64 px`), `2` 5-frame battle sprite strips (`160 × 32 px`), and `2` Courtyard Marks (`16 × 16 px`).
- **Alchemist's Hut, Bombs & Potions Rebalance (`rulesVersion 7 → 8` dual path, `D632`–`D633`):**
  - **Dual `rulesVersion` Preservation:** Historical contract replays (`FightRef`), golden fights, Delve last-floor replays, and `rulesVersion ≤ 7` Last Stands continue replaying under their frozen recorded version bit-for-bit identically. Live gameplay (`rulesVersion 8`) enables the rebalanced math.
  - **Bombs (`prep.bombSlots = 2`, all scale cleanly with Alchemy):**
    - *Fire Pot*: unchanged (`40 + 3 × 10` Fire; cracks *Unthawed*; Well-Prepared on Fire weak).
    - *Frost Pot*: keeps `30` Frost + `−30%` Speed × `3` actions, and **fixes Speed-slow overwriting** so *Frost Pot*, *Rime-Dipped*, and *Hamstring* stack cleanly without clobbering one another.
    - *Saltburst*: recipe cost cut from `2 rime salt + 1 silver` to **`1 carrier + 1 silver`**; deals **`25` Rune damage** (scales with Alchemy), triggers reliably on the monster's **first active OR passive ability**, blocks both active and passive monster abilities for `3` actions, and fixes the Prep UI's Well-Prepared badge.
    - *Poppy Smoke*: stuns the monster's next action AND applies **Shrouded** for its next `2` actions (`+15` points hit chance, `+10` points crit chance, `+15%` damage dealt, scaled by Alchemy). Well-Prepared stays tied to weak elemental bombs and Saltburst while Poppy Smoke wins through raw fight odds & boss burst.
  - **Potions (all 6 scale with *Herbwise* + Alchemy potency):**
    - *Quickfoot Tincture*: `+20% → +15%` Speed for the whole fight.
    - *Owl-Eye Drops*: `+10` pts hit chance **and `+10` pts crit chance** whole fight (uncapped scaling into crits; answers *Lightless* and *Ill Luck*).
    - *Hardwood Tonic*: **`+40%` Armor and `−15%` all damage taken (`taken%`)** until holder's **8th action**.
    - *Mending Draught*: triggers below **`50%` Health** (`65%` with *Herbs* talent), heals **`45%` max Health**.
    - *Coldwater Draught*: **`−50%` Fire & `−30%` Frost damage taken plus `+10%` Might** for the whole fight.
    - *Steadfast Draught*: prevents next **`2` ability-lost actions** (`2–4` scaled by *Herbwise* & Alchemy) **plus `+10%` Might** whole fight.
  - **Alchemist's Hut & Alchemy XP Pacing:** `Alchemist's Hut` keeps `+1` kettle slot per level and gains **`−8%` brew & experiment duration per level above `1`** (`0%` → `−32%` at L5, shown in `Breakdown`); `skill.xp.alchemy` rises from `{ brew: 1, experiment: 3, property: 10 }` to **`{ brew: 4, experiment: 5, property: 10 }`** (tunable) so Alchemy paces naturally past level `4–5` toward `8–10` alongside the other warden skills.
- **General Balance Pass & UI Honesty Fixes (`D634`, `D636`):**
  - `"Why?"` breakdown (`explainFight`) includes start potions, Alchemy potency, and tick-1 bombs; Contract `Suggest` preserves `input.prep` consistently across finalist ranking and returned odds.
  - Buffed weak 40-Riftglass alternate traits (*Dipped Knife* `+30%` Venom, *Thrifty* `−40%` provisions, *Rock-Footed* `+4` flat Armor, *Fjord-born* / *Field-born* `+20%` location Might, *Forge-Eyed* `+8` pts party hit on ruins) and *Emberwright Plate* (`−10% → −25%` Fire damage taken under `rulesVersion 8`).
  - Full simulator calibration (`T19.9`) across Rising Vigil, Descent, Provisions, Eventer boss bounty share, and Alchemy skill pacing, locking updated `sim/baseline.json` once after passing `sim:check`.
