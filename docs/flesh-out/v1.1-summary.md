## 3. What `v1.1 — The Hold's Layout` Builds (D500–D507)

- **The 8 × 8 Grid & Rank Clearings (D500, M1):**  
  - Coordinates `0..7 × 0..7` with **▲ The Fell Breach** along the north edge (row `0`) and **▼ Lowland Gate** along the south edge (row `7`).  
  - **Hamlet** opens `4 × 4` (16 tiles) → **Keep** `6 × 6` (36 tiles) → **Citadel** `8 × 6` (48 tiles, uncovering row 1 across the full 8-tile north front) → **Bastion** `8 × 8` (64 tiles).  
  - **Citadel defenses in row 1 (M1):** Walls 1–6 and Watchtowers 1–2 sit in row `1`, with Wall wings 7–8 in row `2`, fully inside Citadel's clearing so defenses are visible and active immediately when unlocked. Bastion defenses sit in row `0`.  
- **39 Placeable Pieces (42 tiles max, leaving exactly 22 tiles of roads/courtyard, D501):**  
  - **Hearth Hall (`2 × 2` centerpiece, M4):** Anchored inside `2..4 × 2..4` (default `(3, 3)`), ensuring it is always 100% inside the clearing at all Ranks and never displaced or reset across the Long Night.  
  - **8 Unique/Level buildings (`1 × 1`):** Mill, Sawpit, Smithy, Bell Tower, Barracks, Trophy Hall, Hunter's Lodge, Alchemist's Hut (3 visual tiers by level).  
  - **17 Count-building plots (`1 × 1`):** Cottage, Farm, Woodcutter's Camp, Mine, Herb Garden get up to 3 plots each (`1`, `5`, `15` owned); Silver Seam gets up to 2 (`1`, `2` owned). Each plot visually grows from single → pair → cluster as count rises.  
  - **14 Defense pieces (`1 × 1`):** 3 Watchtowers (`Lv 1, 8, 15`) and 11 auto-connecting Wall segments (`4` at Lv 1, `+1` every 2 levels to Lv 15).  
- **Blueprint Persistence ("The stones remember", D502, M2):**  
  - Save schema moves to **12** (`state.layout`). Your layout **survives the Long Night**. Four mutually exclusive piece states (`locked`, `active`, `dormant-ring`, `tray`).  
  - Saved blueprint coordinates are **never overwritten automatically**. Newly unlocked pieces take their saved spots if free; `dormant-ring` pieces wait peacefully without triggering the tray badge.  
  - New games initialize with only `hearth-hall` placed (no spoiler ghost outlines, M3). Migrating v11 saves populates `CANONICAL_LAYOUT`.  
- **Hold Screen & Arrange Hold Sheet (D503, S5, S8):**  
  - The Hold screen shows your live grid by default (smoothly scaled, non-interactive), with a **Hold view** (`Grid` / `Classic scene`) switch in `More ▸ Settings` (stored outside the save).  
  - **[Layout ▸]** opens the **Arrange Hold** sheet: `44 × 44 px` tile buttons, tap-to-select and tap-to-move/swap (no drag needed), Tray, **Auto-arrange**, **Clear to tray**, and **Blueprint ghosts On/Off**. Gated player-facing strings table in GDD §9.21.  
- **Art & Phased Contact Sheet Gate (D504, S2, S3):**  
  - Grid art is split into **TL.5a** (ground/borders/ghosts + `v1.0-hashes.json`), **TL.5b** (defenses/buildings/hall), and **TL.5c** (plots/cosmetics/contact sheet). All ~75 assets use the 32-colour palette. Redraws capped at ≤ 12 with before/after approval.  
- **Zero Rules Risk:** `rulesVersion` stays **5**; all golden fights, recorded stands and simulator baselines remain identical to `v1.0`.
