# OUT-7 — Summary for the owner (v0.10 Orders flesh-out, 2026-10-21; revised after the independent review)

## What was decided

- **Orders are story first, with a cost** (D408): limited seats, small perks, short questlines, no silver, no fight number, no gate. `rulesVersion` stays 5.
- **Standing** comes from play you already do, has four levels (**Neighbour, Friend, Ally, Kin**), never drops and is kept through the Long Night (D410). It is counted for all four Orders from the first v10 save on; **your save gets one backfill, capped at level 1**, so the questlines come one at a time instead of all at once.
- **Seats:** two, and a third once two Orders are at level 4 **and** the Quenchers have come, checked in whatever order those happen (D412). Free swaps at each new warden; a 24 h cooldown mid-cycle.
- **Perks:** one small number and one story perk per level, only while seated (D411). The Quenchers' perk is a **trade**: easier waves but a smaller Embers payout.
- **Sixteen chapters**, four per Order; the Moot's and the Quenchers' last chapters end differently depending on whether the other is seated, and **each waits until the other Order has opened** (D414).
- **The final act is a new milestone, v0.11 "The Remembering"** (D415), between Orders and v1.0.
- **A new simulator player, the Orderer**, with six proposed rows (D417).
- **The decisions sweep** is one new row, D407; no old row is edited.
