# v1.4 "The Descent" — flesh-out summary (2026-10-25; updated 2026-10-27)

For the owner. Plain language. Decisions are **D557–D568** (all start as `Proposed`; **D568** is your yes in the first message of the v1.4 Claude Code thread).

## What v1.4 is

**The Descent (`lore-secrets.md` §6, §34.11, D448, D477, D499): the third ending.** Once the Remembering gate is open (`endingChoosable`: all 40 names found, all 16 contract species Studied, and Act VI's stand applied) and the hold has reached **Bastion rank in the current cycle** (D-01/D-07 and GDD §5.1.3: the door at the foot of the Bastion stair is restored only at Bastion), you can march down the Bastion stair into the Deep for a **three-stage all-hands relay battle** using everything the hold has learned across every cycle. Winning scatters the Unlit into the deep seams (*"A costly, heroic victory. The Unlit is scattered, not ended"*).

On a win, `'descent'` is recorded once in `remembering.endings`, the warden gets a Roll of Wardens mark and *"Led the Descent."*, and the story sequence and all 3 stage fight logs are saved to Library shelf **"The Remembering"** for replay (D558, D563) |

## Readings made by Claude (keep or change at D568; recorded in D567)

1. **Hold Rank Bastion requirement:** because D-01 and D-07 describe the door at the foot of the Bastion stair, which is restored only at Bastion each cycle (GDD §5.1.3), the Descent requires Bastion rank in the current cycle, preventing Hamlet balance swings and lore mismatches (S6).
2. **Running contracts and paused Delves:** because the Descent does not trigger a Long Night, starting the Descent does **not** finish running contracts on the spot or end a paused Delve. Hunters currently away on a contract or in a Delve are simply marked unavailable in the Descent marching-order picker until their contract is collected or the Delve ends.
3. **Mid-stage reserve step-in rules (S1):** when front-line hunters fall, replacements step in before the next stage; if all standing front-line hunters fall *mid-stage* while reserves wait, waiting reserves step in at base turn meter 0 (with start-of-fight meter traits applied as they enter), taking slots in queue order, start-of-fight buffs applying from entry, and bombs not re-thrown.
4. **Trait scopes and retries (M6, S7):** "once per fight" traits are once per Descent per hunter; retries on defeat are free and immediate, with spent consumables lost from stores.
5. **Ability interactions:** Halvar's `Smoke-Lunged` halves Frost drain from The Unlit's `First Cold`; Herdis's `Shepherd's Crook` clears `Heavy Dark`'s healing cut (`Chill`, S14); `Coldwater Draught` reduces Fire only and does not affect `First Cold`.
6. **Hearth Hall look:** winning the Descent does not remove the Hearth Hall frost or light the window lamp (*"The Unlit is scattered, not ended"*, `lore-secrets.md` §6; the warm hearth look belongs to Remember Them, D457).
7. **Sound cues (v1.2):** clearing Stage 1 or Stage 2 plays `win`, beating Stage 3 plays `fanfare`, and losing plays `lose`; story cards D-01 to D-07 are silent (D567 (6)).

## Red-team pass: what could go wrong, and how it is covered

| Risk | Cover |
|---|---|
| **Old fights or stands change.** Group rules, reserve step-ins or The Unlit's abilities leak into ordinary fights | `runDescent` is an isolated fight path; `rulesVersion` stays 5; non-coupling lint test; every golden fight and recorded stand replays line for line; stop and ask if any changes (D561) |
| **`unlit` leaks into Delve guardian draws or Deep bestiary** | `unlit` is flagged `descentOnly: true` (M2); tests enforce byte-identical Delve guardian and floor draws on fixed seeds, unchanged stand pools and gate species (M2) |
| **Replay drift in Library:** contract species knowledge changes after Descent win | `DescentRecord` freezes `bestiaryKnowledge` for every monster across all 3 stages (M3); tests prove logs rebuild identical after state changes |
| **A real CI failure is waved away as a container glitch** (the D553 offline-reload lesson) | Standing rule in `CLAUDE.md`: a failing test must be shown to fail on `main` without the branch's changes before it can ever be called a runner glitch; `icons/` stays unpacked; TD.11 runs offline reload on the production build and checks `sw.js` for duplicate hashes (D557) |
| **Save growth exceeds budget** with full 14-hunter snapshots | Measured in TD.4b from real full 14-hunter records; row proposed via D445/D469 measured-row pattern (sketch ≤ 4 KB for full records; M4) |
| **Fixture or test cannot win honestly** | Shared builder `debug.prepareEndgameRoster()` equips endgame roster; `debug.descent.win()` runs real seeded attempts until an honest victory occurs so `rebuildDescentLogs` replays honestly (M5, M10) |
| **Probe reference state undefined** | Shared builder defines two explicit profiles: minimal gate-open vs maxed endgame (M10); calibration table reports both |
| **Calibration row failure or lack of bound** | Row A bounded by win rate 50%–85% and median 2–8 downed (M9); Row B judged by ≥ 25 percentage points win-rate advantage over zeroed bonus (M9) |
| **Waiting reserves are wasted** if a stage AoE downs all front-line hunters at once | Mid-stage reserve step-in (D567 (3), S1): waiting reserves step in at base turn meter 0 (start-of-fight meter traits apply to base), slot order preserved, entry buffs applied, bombs not re-thrown |
| **Playback interrupted by app close / reload** | `unreadResult: true` and `descent.last` preserved (S9), showing "View Descent result" upon returning |
| **e2e shard passes ceiling** | 8-minute ceiling per shard (D552); stop-and-ask at 7 min 30 s |
| **The `vigil` simulator shard passes 4 minutes** | Calibration grid search runs on-demand via `npm run sim:descent -- ...` (S8); CI checks only the fixed constants in ≤ 4 min |
