# v0.9 event calibration (T9.13): measured numbers

Made by `sim/calibrate-events.ts` (a one-off script, not part of the suite) on 5 seeds. Snapshots are the Eventer's games at four moments; each number is the median over the 5 seeds. Boss damage uses the Eventer's party and kit; tracks use the **best legal party among all owned hunters**, rest and contracts ignored, so they are a ceiling. Win rates are over 300 fights; "(n)" is how many of the 5 seeds had a full legal party.
