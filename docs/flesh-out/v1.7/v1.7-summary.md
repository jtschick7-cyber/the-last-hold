# v1.7 "Steel & Hearth" — summary for the owner (2026-11-04)

## What this milestone does

Milestone v1.7 completes the visual overhaul started in v1.6 across every combat screen, brings optional Arena fight playback to ordinary Contracts alongside punchier step feedback, and adds a zero-file procedural Web Audio frontier ambience soundscape plus three missing short Kenney sound cues — with **zero changes to combat math, economy, save format, or simulator balance**.

- **40 pixel-art battle sprite strips (`D605`, closes `D490`):** Every hunter (14), bestiary species (16), Deep guardian (3), event creature (6), and The Unlit (1) gets a crisp `160 × 32 px` 5-frame battle strip (`idle`, `idle2`, `lunge`, `hit`, `downed`) on a transparent background in the 32-color Zughy palette, matching its v1.6 portrait. Hunters face right, foes face left, and the 3 legendary hunters (*Gest Tall-Torch*, *Radmila of the Reeds*, *Ragnhild Eye-Taker*) receive bespoke `idle2` and `lunge` flame, marsh-ward, and bone-needle animations. Reviewed in **4 batches of 10** (`VB.2`, `VB.4`, `VB.6`, `VB.8`) interleaved with UI and audio tasks.
- **Contract *Watch fight ▸* & Arena polish (`D606`, `D607`):** Contract result rows gain **Watch fight ▸** beside **Fight log ▸** whenever the recent fight record is still in memory, opening the Arena (`{Monster} · T{tier}` with **Skip**, **Log**, and **Done**, showing the earlier-rules note if applicable, and silent at completion per `D522`). Watched Arena steps display floating step callouts (`-{n}`, `★ -{n}`, `+{n}`, `MISS`), a brief 3 px hit recoil shake, warm ember Wright-skill glows, and cold frost/violet ability glows — all gated behind Reduce Motion. Fixes the horizontal double-flip on the monster sprite in Last Stand wave logs.
- **Three new Kenney cues & 45 KB audio sub-cap (`D608`):** Expands shipped cues from 7 sounds / 15 moments to **10 sounds / 19 moments** (picked by ear from ~12 candidates on the preview audition page at `VB.5`): **Stand brace** (`brace`, on tapping **Start** on the Last Stand screen to open confirmation and on **Use this layout**), **Discovery** (`discover`, when a live-tick Alchemy experiment reveals a new property), and **Combat impact** (`strike`, a soft low-gain hit tick during watched Arena playbacks, lowest in the 150 ms priority window and skipped on Skip). Moves GDD §6.5's *"Sound files, total"* sub-cap from `≤ 30 KB` to **`≤ 45 KB`** while keeping the **600 KB (614,400-byte)** whole-build download ceiling unchanged.
- **Procedural Web Audio *Ambience* (`D609`, `D610`):** Adds an independent **Ambience** `[On] [Off]` row in `More ▸ Settings` (outside the save, default **Off**, ~3 KB code / **0 KB** audio files, declared `ambient` so the iPhone silent switch mutes it, paused immediately when hidden, and **faded out to complete silence during The Remembering reading and The Descent story cards**). Shifts smoothly between *Hearth & Wind* on normal tabs and *Night Watch* inside a Delve run, the Last Stand screen, or The Descent arena.

## What does not change

- **Save format:** stays **15** (no migration, no new save fields; `ambience` lives in `lasthold:settings`).
- **Combat rules:** `rulesVersion` stays **6**; every golden fight and recorded version 5 and version 6 stand replays line for line.
- **Simulator:** all **63 checks** and **192 baseline moments** remain unchanged.
- **Routine check-in speed:** stays **≤ 15 taps** (**Watch fight ▸** is optional on Collect).
