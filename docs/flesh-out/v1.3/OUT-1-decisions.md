| D549 | 2026-10-24 | **Readings made by Claude (the docs were silent; the owner keeps or changes each at D551).** (1) **The additive step flattens the curve.** With a steady additive step, each level adds the same amount to the multiplier, so the waves lost per level shrink as the level rises (roughly logarithmically): about one wave at level 1, less later. The strength never stops rising, but the *waves held* fall more slowly. The alternative is a compounding step (`intPow(1 + s, L)`), same percent each level. **This is Q57**; KV.8 reports the measured curve for **both** the additive and the compounding step, and **the owner answers before KV.9 and before any vigil stand is recorded in a save that ships**: switching later would change those stands' replays, which is a combat math change (a `rulesVersion` bump or a formula id on the record). "Decide after the playtest" is therefore not an option. Until then: additive (the owner's "steady steps"). (2) **The Vigilant opens the gate with a debug shortcut** (D546 (2)): it proves the vigil mechanics, not that a real player reaches the gate (the reachability row already does that). **This is Q58.** (3) **The probe is judged in `sim.yml` only** (D546 (4)), a change from "in the fast set", to protect `npm run check`. (4) **The Embers bonus is a few tenths per level at typical Threat** (my rough estimate from the payout formula, not measured; one wave is about a seventh of a stand's wave part at 15 waves). KV.9 measures and proposes `e`. (5) **The row is hidden while the gate is closed** (no teaser). (6) **The switch can be flipped any time before the stand starts, and a flip needs no confirmation** (it is not final; Start is). (7) **A Triumphant stand at any level counts as today** (no extra trust or standing for the vigil). (8) **`stands` is stored although it could be derived** from the records, because the owner approved the block and because a debug set and the "level kept" line read it; the validation warning (D543 (6)) and a test cover drift. (9) **A level counts when the stand starts with the switch On, even if the stand goes badly** (a Broken stand still counts as the level), which is how D539 reads "started with the switch On". (10) **Honest limits found by the outside red-team, written into GDD §5.9.10:** (a) fewer waves held means fewer Triumphant stands (15 or more); a Triumphant stand is worth 10 contracts of trust for every village and `orders.triumphantChunk` (25) of standing for every Order, and drives the residents' band lines, so a player whose trust or Orders are not full is slowed by a high level; (b) Command skill XP is per wave, so fewer waves means less of it (probably full by the gate); (c) the vigil term does not grow with Threat, so its relative effect, s × L ÷ (1 + w × Threat), is smaller the later in the cycle the stand is fought, and the waves lost per level depend on stand timing; (d) fixed-damage sources (the Watchtower's volley, bombs) get relatively weaker and the Dross-Tide's hit-everyone effect relatively stronger. KV.8 reports the curve at two Threat points and the band at each level. (11) **The gate can close** if the pinned contract-species list ever changes (D461); the row then hides with the switch still On, which is harmless (level 0). (12) **Row D** (Off equals level 0) is a unit test, not a simulator row (D546 (5)). (13) **Size fallback order** in D547. | Claude readings | Proposed |
