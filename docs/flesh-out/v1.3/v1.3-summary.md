## One thing to decide (it does not block anything yet)

**Steady steps flatten out.** You chose "steady steps and no ceiling". With a steady step, every level adds the *same amount* of strength. So each new level is a smaller percentage jump than the one before, and the **waves held fall more slowly the higher you go**: about one wave lost at level 1, less at level 6, less again later. The strength never stops rising, but a player just flipping it On will see the wave count drop in smaller and smaller steps. (This is my reasoning from the formula, not a measurement.)

The alternative is a **compounding step**: every level is the same *percentage* harder, which loses about one wave at every level.

**What I did:** kept your choice (steady). The simulator measures the real curve for **both** kinds of step in task KV.8, and you answer **Q57** after seeing the numbers and **before** the numbers are set (KV.9). It cannot wait until the playtest: once a vigil stand is recorded in a save, switching the formula would change how that stand replays, which counts as a combat math change.

## What v1.3 is

**Rising Vigil: an On / Off switch on the Last Stand screen.** It appears only once the Remembering gate is open (40 names found, every contract species Studied, Act VI's stand done). It stays available after Remember Them. The ending choice "Keep the Vigil" still changes nothing.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does a level do? | Wave Health and Might go up by one more step per level (one lever only), and the Embers go up a little per level |
| What is a level? | The number of stands you have already fought with the switch On, plus one. First On stand = level 1 |
| What does Off do? | An ordinary stand, exactly as today. Your level is kept for next time |
| Does the switch survive the Long Night? | Yes, it stays as you left it |
| Is there a ceiling? | No |
| What is the target? | About **one wave fewer held per level** at the Standard player's median, and **Embers at levels 1 to 3 about level with an ordinary stand** (D540, D541) |
| What never changes? | Cycles 1 to 6, contracts, Delves, events, Threat rates, band thresholds, relic milestones, every other simulated player's numbers |
| Where does it show? | One row on the stand screen; two extra lines in the preview; a line in the confirmation and the result; a small mark and a line on the Roll of Wardens |
| New art, story text, sound? | None |

## Readings I made (the docs were silent; keep or change at D551)

- The switch can be flipped any time before the stand starts, with no confirmation (Start is the final step).
- A Triumphant stand at any level counts for trust and Orders standing exactly as today (no extra). **But a high level makes Triumphant stands rarer** (they need 15 waves), which slows trust and Orders for a player who has not filled them. Said plainly in the GDD, along with: less Command skill XP (per wave), the vigil matters less late in a cycle, and the Watchtower and bombs count for relatively less.
- The Embers line is a whole percent of the base 1, rounded down, so it adds up with the Threat line.
- A level counts when the stand starts with the switch On, even if the stand then goes badly (a Broken stand still counts).
- A stand started with the switch On uses the level saved on its record, for ever: a replay never uses the switch's current state.
- The name "Rising Vigil" is two plain words; "Vigil" is already in the game. A web search found one unrelated board-game expansion with "Vigil" in its title and no game called "Rising Vigil". The search tool is weak for exact phrases, so this means "no collision found". If you dislike it, the fallback is "Harder stands". (OUT-6.)
