## What we decided (D357–D387)

**World bosses.**
- Three Great Beasts, one at a time, in a fixed order: the **Lindworm of Velk Fjord**, the **Barrow-King**, the **Mother of the River**. After the third, they come round again one tier higher.
- A shared Health pool, sized so a visit takes about **12 attempts**. A party is up to **4** hunters. The attempt is worked out at once and played back with Skip and Log; nothing runs while you're away.
- **Tokens** bank by real time, **1 per 8 h up to 6**, and the bank starts empty when events open. A full bank shows a **badge on the Hold tab**, only while events are open.
- A hunter who attends **rests 24 h from other events**, but can still take contracts and Delves. Injured hunters stay out of events; the Last Stand is the exception.
- Rewards come at **25%, 50%, 75% and the kill**: a little silver, reagents, and one item at the kill. Attendees earn hunter XP (reserves too). **Yard XP stays contracts-only.** No injuries, no deaths.
- The tier is fixed by your Rank when a visit's first attempt is made (Citadel 4, Bastion 5). A preview shows the expected damage from 100 practice fights.

**Legendary Hunts.**
- Three hunts, each with a quarry, three tracks and one legendary hunter. A track limits who can come. One attempt is one fight and costs one token; a win pays more points than a loss, so nothing is wasted. **Points carry over, even through the Long Night.** 100 points unlocks the hunter, who joins free, wearing first-tier Common pieces made for them.
- Stoneback the Fell Troll gives **Gest Tall-Torch**; the Mara of Kettle Barrow gives **Radmila of the Reeds**; Likho the One-Eyed gives **Ragnhild the Eye-Taker**. None has letters; none is Yrsa.
- A new **Great Beasts** shelf in the Archive holds the bosses and quarries. It is optional.

**Where it lives.** An **Events card on the Hold screen** under the Rift card, opening at the first Citadel with a short scene. No new tab.

**The Dross-Tide** joins the Last Stand in every stand made once Act VI has begun, flagged on the cycle record, shown by name when it appears, with no wave guaranteed, and counting nowhere. Every old stand and fight replays as before.

**Act VI, The Unlit, is written** (OUT-3): ten documents. The cold writes in frost by the crack, a new **Rime** type with its own shelf. The three names (Milena Horská, Ketil Ormsson, Mirek Bárta) arrive on the Keep, Citadel and Bastion. Halina, Arvid and Sigrun each get a short closing scene. A new Accounts event, *What the cold wants*. Act VI leads toward The Remembering; it doesn't write it.

**The sweep.** One new row (D385) records that every old Proposed row was already accepted by a later row, and that D337 (2)–(10) are accepted today. Old rows are not edited. D186 raises nothing new.

**Saves.** Schema **v9**; your real save is the fixture.

**Simulator.** A new **Eventer** player (Monday seeds only) in its own shard. Five approved rows, two proposed ones (Act VI lands in its cycle; the Dross-Tide stand probe). All judged on the median of the Monday seeds.

**Size.** 19 tasks (T9.1–T9.19), about **10–15 sessions**. This estimate rests on v0.8's actual session count, which wasn't visible here; Claude Code adds it and revises.
