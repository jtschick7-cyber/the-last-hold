| D581 | 2026-10-28 | **Walls: cover and depth (owner's answers, Questions 3 (b) and the depth gap (a)).** (1) **Front zone:** only wall and tower pieces that sit **on the grid in rows 0 to 2** count (`breach.frontRows` = 3, locked). A piece in the Tray, in a locked ring, or in rows 3 to 7 gives nothing. (2) **Cover of lane c** = `breach.coverOwn` (0.5) if column c holds at least one counted piece, plus `breach.coverNeighbour` (0.25) for each neighbouring column (c − 1, c + 1) that does. **The ruined edge counts as walled:** a missing neighbour past column 0 or 7 counts as present. So own column plus one neighbour is 75%, all three is 100%. (3) **Depth:** each extra counted piece in the same column adds `breach.depthStep` (0.25, tunable) to that lane's wall cut, up to `breach.depthMax` (2, tunable) extra pieces, and only where the column has a piece. (4) **Wall cut for a wave in lane c** = `walls.takenPerLevel × Walls level × cover(c) × (1 + depthStep × extra(c))`, added into the existing taken% bucket (so the 75% cap holds). With cover 100% and no depth it equals today's `2% × level`. Towers count as pieces for cover and depth. (5) Economy buildings that sit on front-row tiles can be moved off them (to make room for a third piece in a column), so moving them is a real tactic; Suggest uses only free tiles and never moves another building. Calibration includes this. | Owner (Q3 (b); depth gap (a)); Claude drafts numbers | Proposed |
| D582 | 2026-10-28 | **Watchtowers: reach and shared volley (owner's answer, Question 4 (b), Question 10).** (1) Today's total volley (`watchtower.volleyPerLevel` 20 × Watchtower level) is **shared among the counted towers**, the towers you have placed on the grid in the front rows (owner's answer to red-team SF-1: dividing by towers owned would make an upgrade at Watchtower levels 8 and 15 weaker in some lanes until the new tower is placed; this way an upgrade never lowers a lane). The cost, stated plainly: one well-placed tower already gives the full volley in its own lane, so extra placed towers spread the cover over more lanes and never add damage to one lane. (2) Each **counted** tower's share for a wave in lane c is `breach.towerReach[d]` where d = the distance in columns from the tower's column to c: full (1) in its own column, half (0.5) one column away, a quarter (0.25) two away, nothing beyond. (3) **Volley for lane c** = `20 × Watchtower level × breach.volleyScale × (Σ over counted towers of reach(d)) ÷ (number of counted towers)`, and **0 when no tower counts** (also at Watchtower level 0). It is worked in whole numbers so a hand-worked table and the code agree: reach in quarters (4, 2, 1), the scale in hundredths, multiplied first, divided once, rounded down (technical design §23.2); `towerReach` stays in multiples of 0.25 and `volleyScale` in hundredths. Still Steel damage at tick 1 that ignores Armor and takes the species' Steel resistance, after the potions and before the bombs (D120). A tower in the Tray gives nothing and does not dilute the others. (4) With all counted towers in the wave's own column and `volleyScale` 1, the volley equals today's. | Owner (Q4 (b)); Claude drafts numbers | Proposed |
