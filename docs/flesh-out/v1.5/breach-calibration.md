## 6. What the table says, and the proposed values

**What I found.**
- **The layout matters, but only a little at the first stand.** From no Walls and no towers to Walls 15 and Watchtower 15 is worth about 2 waves in all (15 against 17). Placing pieces well or badly moves the result by well under that: the bad layout loses about 0.5 wave at mid and about 1.5 waves at max, against Auto-arrange.
- **Towers hold almost nothing at this stand.** Taking every tower off the grid changes the waves held by less than a tenth of a wave (section 3). Moving economy buildings off the front rows changes nothing (section 4).
- **Auto-arrange is as good as today.** It is within 0.15 waves of the version 5 rules at all three points and holds 15 to 17 waves, so row A (12 or more and within 1 wave) passes with room.
- **The Tactician does not beat Auto-arrange.** It is level with it (within 0.12 waves, sometimes below) at the first stand, and ahead by 0.14 at the third stand at max. Row B (at least 1 wave more) is out of reach.
- **The bad layout does not lose 2 waves.** At most 1.5 (max point, first stand). Row C (at least 2 fewer) is out of reach at every point.
- **No tunable closes the gap.** Across every value tried (volley scale up to 4, depth step up to 0.5, depth limit up to 3, tower reach up to [1, 1, 1], four cover splits, a Suggest window of 8 to 24), the best layout never gains more than 0.3 wave and the bad layout never loses more than 1.5. The 75% taken cap never binds (0.0%), so depth has room.
- **The third stand (section 1b) behaves the same way**, a little more layout-sensitive (the bad layout loses 1.1 waves at max against Auto-arrange) but nowhere near 2.

**Proposed values (for TB.13, if you approve).** Change one value, `breach.volleyScale` 1 → 3 (a tunable moved by 3×, so it needs its own Proposed row at TB.13). Keep every other placeholder as it is.
- **Why 3:** at 1 a well-placed tower pair volleys about a third of today's flat volley (max point: about 94 against 300). At 3 the average comes back to today's (about 281 against 300), so the Breach takes nothing away from a player who places towers well. With 3 the full simulator suite passes all 60 rows and 191 of the 192 moments equal the recorded baseline; the one that moves is the Orderer's first Long Night, 99.0 h → 101.5 h (later; it had moved earlier at TB.11a), within its row.
- **Why the rest stay:** cover 50/25, depth 25% up to two extra, reach [1, 0.5, 0.25] and a 12-wave Suggest window give Auto-arrange the same strength as today (mean wall cut 35.6% against 30% at max, 16.5% against 16% at mid, 1.3% against 2% at low), and no other value in the grid changes the waves by more than 0.3.
- **Rising Vigil rows (D546, tuned under version 5):** `vigil-level-rises`, `vigil-never-more-waves` and `vigil-embers-level` all pass with the proposed value.

**What this means for the three rows (Q63).** Row A can be committed as written. Rows B and C cannot pass with any value in the Breach family. See the options in `docs/questions.md`, Q63.
