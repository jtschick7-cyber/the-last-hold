# v1.5 "The Breach" — summary for the owner (2026-10-28, revised after the red-team review)

## What this milestone does
Where you put Walls and Watchtowers now matters in the Last Stand. Each wave comes down one of the 8 lanes (the 8 columns of the Fell Breach). The lane is fixed by the stand's seed and shown in the wave list from Bastion. Only walls and towers **on the grid in the three rows nearest the Breach** count. A piece in the Tray gives nothing. **Nothing in the Last Stand places a piece for you** (Start, the preview, a load and the migration never move anything). v1.1's existing auto-placement of newly unlocked pieces on purchase, Rank-up and a new cycle stays as it is.

- **Walls:** today's 2% per level, scaled by the lane's **cover**. A wall in the lane's own column gives half the cover, and a wall in each neighbouring column gives a quarter. The ruined edge counts as walled. **Stacking** more walls in one column adds 25% each, up to two extra.
- **Watchtowers:** today's volley is **shared among the towers you have placed in the front rows**. Each placed tower reaches a lane fully in its own column, half one column away, a quarter two away. With none placed the volley is 0.
- **Placement screen:** a **Lanes strip** of 8 boxes (two rows of four on a phone), shown from Bastion on. Tap a lane to see why; on the Arrange Hold sheet its covering pieces light up. A text list is the alternative. The Last Stand screen shows the same strip read-only, with an **Arrange Hold** button. **Suggest layout** (on the sheet) shows a good arrangement and places nothing.
- **Old stands and saves:** old stands replay exactly as before. Save format goes 14 → 15 (trivial migration). Your layout is not changed.
- **What does not read layout:** the Descent, contracts, Delves, events, bosses, hunts, Rising Vigil's steps, the Embers formula, the Threat rate. **But** layout changes the waves you hold, so it indirectly changes Embers paid, relics, the Held and Triumphant bands, trust, Orders standing and Command XP. The plan now says so and reports it.
