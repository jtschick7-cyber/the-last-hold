# v1.8 "Ward & Rampart" — summary for the owner (2026-11-13)

## What this milestone does

Milestone v1.8 turns the Hold Grid from a small `8 × 8` diorama with only front-row defense math into an **Age of Empires + Clash of Clans** inspired `8 × 12` tactical stronghold — while adding **Hold Look** toggleable **Courtyard Marks** (closing `D490`) and refreshing the grid's tile art in the unified 32-color Zughy palette.

- **Expanded `8 × 12` Portrait Castle (`D618`):** Expands the Hold Grid from `8 × 8` (`64` tiles) to **`8 × 12` (`96` tiles, `53` open tiles at Bastion)** and widens early Rank clearings (`6 × 6` Hamlet → `8 × 8` Keep → `8 × 10` Citadel → `8 × 12` Bastion). Keeping `8` columns (`8 × 44 px = 352 px` in Arrange Hold) avoids horizontal phone scrolling at `360–390 px` while adding real courtyard and rampart depth.
- **Age of Empires District Adjacency (`D619`, `1.0×` floor + upside):** Orthogonal neighbor links grant transparent `+X%` bonuses shown live in Arrange Hold and in every resource's `Breakdown`: Farms next to **Mill**, Woodcutters next to **Sawpit**, Mines and Silver Seams next to **Smithy**, Herb Gardens next to **Alchemist's Hut**, Cottages near **Hearth Hall** / **Bell Tower**, and **Trophy Hall** beside Hearth Hall or **Barracks**. Poor placement never drops below today's `1.0×` baseline.
- **Clash of Clans Base Geometry (`D618`–`D620`, `rulesVersion` `6 → 7` dual path):**
  - Widens `breach.frontRows` from **`3` → `5`** (`y = 0..4`; `breach.lanes = 8` stays locked at `8`).
  - **North-Reach `Warded` Core:** Traces reachability southward from the Fell Breach edge (`y = 0`) across open tiles; buildings sealed behind continuous `wall-*` / `watchtower-*` ramparts are **Warded** (slowing hourly Threat when **Hearth Hall** is Warded and granting an inner-ward stability uplift). Buildings stay completely free of permanent damage or repair chores (`D108`).
  - **Connected Walls & *Screened Volley*:** Linked `wall-*` tiles (`wallConnectionMask`) add rampart cover strength, any **Watchtower** in rows `0..4` with a connected Wall directly north (`y - 1`) fires a **Screened Volley**, and **Barracks** / **Hunter's Lodge** lend gentle **Sector Support** to nearby columns.
  - **Legacy replay preserved:** Pre-`v1.8` stands (`rulesVersion` `5` and `6`, Arrival stands, and all golden fights) replay byte-for-byte identically. New Bastion stands freeze an extended `v7` table when **Start** is pressed.
- **Save Schema `15 → 16`, Presets & Smart `Auto-Arrange` (`D621`):** Migrates existing `8 × 8` layouts cleanly to `8 × 12` (keeping `y = 0..2` front defenses in place while shifting `hearth-hall` and inner buildings southward into the inner courtyard), adds **`2` custom Layout Presets**, and upgrades **Auto-Arrange** / **Suggest** with **Balanced**, **Economy**, and **Fortress** priorities.
- **Permanent Event *Courtyard Marks* (`D622`, closes `D490`) & *Hold Look* Drawer:** Defeating each of the **3 Great Beasts** and clearing each of the **3 Legendary Hunts** permanently unlocks `1` distinct courtyard keepsake (*Fjord-Bone Standard*, *Barrow Brazier*, *River-Reed Basin*, *Stoneback Cairn*, *Kettle-Barrow Wreath*, *Watch-Fire Sconce*) preserved across Long Night resets (with backfill on load for already-cleared events). Players toggle unlocked Courtyard Marks and bought Riftglass Shop `COSMETIC_IDS` individually in a **Hold Look** drawer rendered in `HoldGrid` and echoed in `HoldScene`.
- **Cohesive Zughy-32 Grid Art Refresh (`D623`):** Hybrid authoring pass redrawing Hearth Hall, all `8` level buildings, all `5` plot/seam archetypes, Watchtower, core Wall kit (with deterministic 16-mask wall/border variants), clearing ground, and the `6` Courtyard Marks across **3 review batches of `≤ 12` (`D475`)**, hash-frozen in `art-src/v1.8-grid-hashes.json`, keeping the whole production build inside **≤ 614,400 bytes (`600 KB`)** gzipped while updating `D615`'s packed-PNG ceiling cleanly in `T18.11`.
